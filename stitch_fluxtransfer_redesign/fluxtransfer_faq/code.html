General

FluxTransfer is a high-performance, security-focused peer-to-peer file transfer service. It allows you to share files directly between devices over your local network or the internet without storing them on intermediary cloud servers, ensuring maximum privacy and speed.

No. FluxTransfer uses a peer-to-peer (P2P) architecture. Your files go directly from the sender's device to the receiver's device. We do not store your files on any cloud servers, providing a "digital vault" level of security for your data.

Yes! FluxTransfer is designed for seamless cross-platform sharing. You can transfer files between iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux devices effortlessly, provided they have the app installed or can access the web interface.

Transfers

Because transfers happen peer-to-peer, there is theoretically no file size limit. You are only limited by the available storage space on the receiving device.
